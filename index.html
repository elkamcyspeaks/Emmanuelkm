Why households switch

Freshness is a supply chain problem. We just solved it.

Most produce sits in distribution centers for 5–9 days before it reaches a store shelf. We cut out that middle stretch entirely.

Picked at peak

Every item is harvested within a day of packing, at the point growers consider it ready — not before, for shelf life.

40 regional growers

We route each order from the closest available farm, so nothing travels further than it has to.

Compostable, always

Boxes, liners and labels break down in a home compost bin — no plastic clamshells, ever.
