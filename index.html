EK

I'm a product designer and creative technologist working across UI/UX, web development, and applied AI. Most days that means shaping interface systems for financial products — clear flows, honest data, interactions people trust. Most nights it means prototyping the tools and generative experiments that make the next project faster.

I care about the seam between craft and system: the point where a good idea stops being a one-off and becomes a component someone else can reuse.
